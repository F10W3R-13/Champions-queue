# :loudspeaker: Update Log — Inactivity MMR
Everything below is running in **observation mode** for now — nothing changes for you yet until we switch it on.

## :hourglass_flowing_sand: Inactive Players Lose MMR Over Time

> Go **7 days without a match** and your MMR starts to slowly drop, speeding up the longer you're away. It never falls below **700**, so there's always a floor to climb back from.
>
> This keeps long-idle accounts from sitting on high ranks over players who actually play.

## :balance_scale: Tournament Players vs Everyone Else

> **🏆 Tournament-team players** decay at the normal rate — 7 days grace, then a steady daily drop.
>
> **👤 Everyone else** gets a gentler deal: **21 days** grace (3× longer) and half the daily loss. If you can't get into the champs-only queue and the general queue is empty, the system accounts for that.

## :no_entry: Under 800 MMR = Queue Paused (Tournament Players Only)

> Tournament players below **800** get queue access paused — restored automatically once you climb back. You'll be DM'd either way.
>
> Everyone else keeps full general-queue access regardless of MMR. *(New accounts under 5 games are exempt from all of the above.)*

## :zzz: Dead Queue Days = No Decay

> On days with **zero matches**, nobody loses MMR — and quiet stretches extend everyone's grace window by the same number of days.
